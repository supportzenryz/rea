Privacy & cookies

This site belongs to Sundar Rajan, fashion photographer (contact: work.photosbysundar@gmail.com). This page explains what it collects and why. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

If you choose Accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and _ga_…, kept for up to two years) to count visits: which pages are seen, roughly where from (country and city, worked out from your connection, never your exact location), the kind of device and browser, and how you arrived (for example a search or a link). It is used only to understand visits to this site; nothing is used for advertising, and the data is kept for 14 months.

If you choose Decline, no cookies are set. Google may still receive anonymous, cookieless signals (Google Consent Mode) that cannot identify you or follow you between visits.

Google acts as a processor for this; see Google's privacy policy.

The contact form

What you send (name, email, the kind of enquiry and your message) is emailed to Sundar Rajan and kept for up to 12 months so he can reply, then deleted. It is used only to answer you.

Hosting and security

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes connection details (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect it. Your choice about cookies is remembered in your browser (sr-consent), not on a server.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what this site holds about you, or withdraw consent at any time, by writing to work.photosbysundar@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.
